Welcome, Readers!

ORV most commonly stands for Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint is a South Korean web novel written by singNsong. It was first published on January 6, 2018, on the platform Munpia, and ended on February 2, 2020

Office worker Kim Dokja is the sole reader who finishes a long, obscure web novel titled Ways of Survival. The real world suddenly turns into the apocalyptic plot of that exact novel. Using his secret knowledge of how the story ends, Dokja tries to survive and change the fate of the world alongside the original fictional hero.
